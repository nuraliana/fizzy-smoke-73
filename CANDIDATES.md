# Candidate Pitches

Scored out of 25: absurdity (10) + relatability (10) + one-sentence craft (5).
All candidates are a single sentence of 220 characters or fewer.

## 1. 24/25 — CHOSEN

> A smoke detector that saves its low-battery chirps for a polite daytime appointment, then apologizes in advance for the inconvenience.

Universal 3 a.m. chirp trauma, flipped into absurdly courteous scheduling.

## 2. 21/25

> A fridge that judges your midnight snacking out loud, then mails you a formal letter of disappointment every Monday morning.

Strong relatable shame, but the letter step slightly dilutes the punch.

## 3. 20/25

> A washing machine that refuses to start until you apologize to the sock it lost last week.

Absurd negotiation with an appliance, but the lost-sock joke is well worn.

## 4. 20/25

> A coffee mug that files a noise complaint against you every time you sip too loudly at your own desk.

Funny inversion of office etiquette, though the premise needs a beat to land.

## 5. 19/25

> A keyboard that quietly swaps two keys each morning so you never get too comfortable typing.

Delightfully petty sabotage, but the absurdity leans more annoying than inventive.

## 6. 19/25

> A mirror that only shows your reflection after a short motivational speech about how great you look today.

Warm and weird, yet the joke resolves a little too kindly for an absurd pitch.

## 7. 18/25

> A pillow that logs your dreams overnight and emails you a performance review before breakfast.

Great concept, but crams two inventions into one sentence and loses crispness.

## 8. 17/25

> A toothpaste tube that dispenses exactly one pea-sized dollop, then locks itself for the rest of the day.

Relatable over-squeeze frustration, but the payoff feels more like a rule than an invention.
